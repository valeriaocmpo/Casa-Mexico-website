Our story

Marco and Silvia came to Ottawa from Acapulco. Their first café, Passport Café, became Casa México.

They opened a cozy spot where people could enjoy the flavours of Mexico, then grew to Preston and Kanata. The goal hasn't changed: good food, a fun room, and everyone feeling at home.
